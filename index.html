About Me

So, who am I? I'm Aditya Valaki — a final-year B.Tech in Information Technology student at G H Patel College of Engineering & Technology (GCET), Anand. Born and raised in Gujarat, India, I've been the kind of person who takes a working laptop apart just to see how it ticks — and I never really stopped.

My journey into tech didn't start with a grand plan. It started with curiosity — wondering how Netflix knew what I'd watch next, how spam filters quietly sorted my inbox, and how a few lines of Python could tell a story hidden inside a messy CSV. That curiosity slowly turned into a serious love for Data Science, Machine Learning, and Analytics, and along the way I picked up Full-Stack Web Development, Computer Vision, and Cybersecurity too.

I'm not the person who learns a framework just to add it to a resume. I learn things because I want to build something — a malware classifier that turns binaries into images, an expense tracker that actually understands how I spend, a watch-party app for friends scattered across cities. Most of my projects start as a small "wait, what if?" and end up on my GitHub.

Outside of code, I lead as the Marketing & Sales Head at Zeeniith Technology, where I get to mix engineering instincts with real business decisions. I've also served as Joint Treasurer & Branch Coordinator at IEEE GCET SB and as an IEEE R10 ACEI Entrepreneurship Ambassador — roles that taught me that good engineers also need to be good communicators and good humans.

When I'm not in front of a screen, you'll find me sketching out product ideas in a notebook, mentoring juniors, exploring new cafes, or losing arguments about football. I'm currently looking for internships and entry-level roles in Data Analytics / ML where I can keep learning, keep shipping, and hopefully build something that actually matters. 👋
